Things to Do in Manali

Manali is a hill town known for mountains and snow. This guide covers things to do in Manali for every budget.

Best Places to Visit in Manali

Solang Valley, Hadimba Temple and Old Manali are popular spots.

Manali Trip Budget

A 4-day trip can cost around ₹10,000 to ₹18,000 depending on your stay and food.

Best Time to Visit Manali

March to June is good for sightseeing, and December to February is best for snow. Roads can close after heavy snowfall, so check the weather before you travel.

Food to Try in Manali

Try Siddu, trout fish and Himachali dham. Many cafes in Old Manali serve good food with mountain views.
