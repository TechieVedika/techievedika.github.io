Things to Do in Goa

Goa is famous for beaches and food. This guide covers things to do in Goa for every budget.

Best Beaches in Goa

Baga and Calangute are lively. Palolem is calm and good for relaxing.

Goa Trip Budget

A 3-day trip can cost around ₹8,000 to ₹15,000 depending on your stay and food.

Best Time to Visit Goa

November to February is the best time to visit Goa. The weather is cool and the beaches are lively. Monsoon months have fewer tourists and lower hotel prices.

Food to Try in Goa

Try fish curry rice, prawn dishes and bebinca, a famous Goan dessert. Beach shacks serve fresh seafood at fair prices.
